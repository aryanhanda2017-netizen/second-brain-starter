# Job name

> One page per job you're running, for a client or for the business: a kitchen fit, a website build, a new van. Copy this page for each job and rename the copy, like `sharma-kitchen.md`. Anything in brackets is an example: write over it.

## Now
Where the job stands today. When something changes, rewrite that line.

- **What it is:** (Example: a new kitchen, supplied and fitted.)
- **Who it's for:** (Example: `pages/clients/sharma-family.md`, or "the business".)
- **What's agreed:** (Example: $18,500, finished by 18 December.)
- **Where it stands:** (Example: deposit paid; the units arrive on 4 December.)

## Open
What happens next, and who does it. The next thing goes first. Take it out when it's done.

- Me: (Example: book the worktop templating for 5 December.)
- Them: (Example: clear the kitchen by 6 December.)

## Log
What happened, one dated line each, oldest first. Only ever add to it, so you can always look back.

- **2026-11-22** | Me: (Example: quote accepted; deposit paid.)
