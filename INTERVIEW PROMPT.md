# The interview prompt

Open a new chat in Claude or ChatGPT. Copy everything in the grey box below (on GitHub, the copy button is in its top right corner), paste it in and send it.

Then answer the questions out loud if you can. Tap the microphone and talk, the way you'd explain it to a friend. It takes about 20 minutes.

Already have a rules page? Paste it right under the prompt in the same message, and the interview carries on from it.

```text
Interview me about my business, one question at a time, and turn my answers into my rules page: the first page of my second brain.

WHY THIS WORKS
How my business really works lives in my head: the decisions only I make. Writing them down is hard, because I don't know what I know until someone asks. So you ask, I answer, and you write each answer down as a rule.

HOW TO RUN IT
1. If I pasted a rules page under this prompt, read it and skip to step 3, starting with the part I want to add to (ask me which). Otherwise, first ask me in one short message what my business sells and who it sells to. Nothing else in that message.
2. Then tell me in two short lines how this works (about 20 minutes, one question at a time, I can answer out loud), and ask your first real question.
3. Go through these parts of my business, in this order: money, clients, pricing, the quality of the work, saying no. Get about two decisions from each part.
4. For each decision, ask these three questions, one per message, in this order:
   a. "What did you decide [this week / lately] about [the part] that nobody else could have?" If I can't think of one, ask about the last time it came up, and give me one everyday example to jog my memory.
   b. "How did you know what to do?"
   c. "When would you have done it differently?"
5. If an answer is vague, ask once for the last real time it happened. If my reason is only "experience" or "gut feel", ask once what went wrong, or would go wrong, if I didn't do it. Then move on.
6. If I answer more than one question at once, use it and skip what I've already answered. An exception I mention at any point counts.
7. After the three answers, write the decision as one rule, in this shape:
   When [the situation], I [what I do], because [my reason].
   If I gave a real exception (a case where I'd do something else, not just the opposite of the situation), add a second sentence: "Except when [exception]: then I [what I do instead]."
   Show me the rule and ask: "Is that right?" If I correct it, rewrite it once and move on.
8. File each rule under the part it fits best, even if we were on another part. Never write the same rule twice.
9. After every second rule, tell me where we are, like: "That's 4 rules. About 6 to go."
10. Stop at 10 new rules, or sooner if I say "done" or run out. Before you give me the page, always ask: "Anything else to add, in any part?" Give me the page only after I say no.

RULES FOR YOU
- One question per message. Keep every message under 40 words, except when you show me a rule, and the finished page.
- Use my words. Never add a rule, a number, a reason or a detail I didn't give you. If you're not sure what I meant, ask.
- Plain words. No business jargon. Don't praise my answers or sum them up; just ask the next question.
- Don't ask for passwords, bank details or anyone's private information. If I mention some, leave it out of the page.

A GOOD RULE LOOKS LIKE THIS
When a client has paid late before, I ask for half upfront, because chasing the money costs me more than the job.

THE PAGE
When we're done, give me the whole page in one block I can copy, in exactly this format:

# My rules

Read this page before you do anything for my business. Follow these rules. When a case isn't covered, use my reasons (the "because" parts) to decide, and if it's still unclear, ask me.

## My business
[One or two lines: what I sell and who to, in my words.]

## Money
1. [rule]
2. [rule]

## Clients
3. [rule]

## Pricing
## Quality
## Saying no
[Same for each part. Number the rules straight through. Leave out a part if it has no rules.]

After the page, tell me in two short lines: save it as rules.md in the "Your rules" folder, and run this prompt again any time to add more rules (paste the page under the prompt and you'll carry on from it).

IF I PASTED A RULES PAGE UNDER THIS PROMPT
Don't ask what my business sells again. Don't repeat a rule I already have. Put each new rule under its part, renumber the whole page straight through, and give me the whole updated page, old rules included, word for word.

Start now with your first question.
```
