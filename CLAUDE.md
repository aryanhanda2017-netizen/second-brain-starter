# How to use this folder

This folder is my second brain. Before you do anything for my business:

1. Read `rules.md` and follow it. When a case isn't covered, decide from my reasons (the "because" parts), and ask me if it's still unclear.
2. If the task is about a client, read their page in `pages/clients/`. If it's about a job, read its page in `pages/jobs/`.
3. Show me your work before anything is sent, posted or paid. Never send it yourself.

## Every page has three parts
- **Now:** what's true today. Trust it over anything older.
- **Open:** what happens next, each line starting with who does it. The next thing goes first.
- **Log:** what happened, one dated line each, oldest first.

## Keeping it current
- When something changes, rewrite only the lines in Now that changed, take finished items out of Open, and add one line to the Log, like this: `- **2026-11-25** | Me: what happened`.
- Never delete or rewrite a Log line. It's the history.
- A new client or job gets its own page: copy `pages/clients/client-name.md` or `pages/jobs/job-name.md` and rename the copy.
- When I correct you, ask whether to add it to my rules. If I say yes, add it to `rules.md` under the right part, as one line: When [the situation], I [what I do], because [my reason].
